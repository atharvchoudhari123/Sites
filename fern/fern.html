LinguaPath — Learn a New Language One Conversation at a Time

LinguaPath is a free language learning platform combining short audio-first lessons, native speaker dialogues, and writing practice for learners from absolute beginner through advanced fluency.

Languages Offered

Active courses include Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Mandarin Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Dutch, Swedish, and English as a Second Language.

Proficiency Levels

Lessons are mapped to the Common European Framework of Reference, covering A1 and A2 beginner, B1 and B2 intermediate, and C1 advanced bands.

Test Prep

Targeted preparation tracks support language proficiency exams and standardized language assessments.

How Lessons Work

Each lesson opens with a contextual dialogue, introduces new vocabulary, drills pronunciation, and ends with a written or spoken exercise.

Conversation Practice

Learners can practice conversations and communicate with other language learners.
